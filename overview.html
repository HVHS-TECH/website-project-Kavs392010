Overview

Location

  • South Korea, officially known as the Republic of Korea (ROK), is a country in East Asia.
  • Ancient & Imperial History: Early kingdoms included Gojoseon (4th century BC), Three Kingdoms (Silla, Baekje, Goguryeo), and the unified Silla Kingdom.
  • It constitutes the southern half of the Korean Peninsula and borders North Korea along the Korean Demilitrized Zone.
  • It is surrounded by the Yellow Sea in the west and the Sea of Japan in the east. The country claims to be the legitimate government of the entire peninsula and adjacent islands.

Cities (Overview)

  • Seoul is the capital and largest city of South Korea, serving its political, economic, and cultural hub. The Seoul Metropolitan Area nearly holds half of the population of the country.
  • Other major cities include the port city Busan, Incheon (known for its international airport), Daegu, Daejeon, Gwangju, Ulsan, and the administrative city Sejong.

Geography

  • South Korea is a mountainous, 750 mile long peninsula in East Asia located at 38°N latitude, bordering North Korea to the north.
  • It roughly covers 100,000 km square, about 70% of the land is mountainous, with major ranges like the Taebaek running north-south while the west and south feature flat plains, valleys, and roughly 3,000 islands.

Basic Facts

  1. The population of South Korea is approximately 51.6 - 51.7 million people.
  2. The currency of South Korea is Korean won (₩) and 1 NZD is 874.76 South Korean won.
  3. The official language of South Korea is Korean which is Hangul writing style.
  4. Major exporters of cars, electronics, and semiconductors are the main contribution of South Korea’s economy. It’s the 4th largest economy in Asia and 13th in the world.
  5. South Korea is known for Kimchi (national dish), Confucian, Buddhist, and Christian traditions, and rapid modernization alongside traditional life.
  6. The life expectancy is high which is expected to be around 84.64 years. Females tend to live longer than males, with recent data showing a substantial longevity advantage. As per the data women's life expectancy is 85.6 years and male’s is 79.9 years.
  7. They also have over 95% literacy rate which is one of the highest educational standards in the world.
